# Mars: the Red Rider, the Smith of the Will

## Core
Will, desire, courage, heat. The force that moves toward.

## Gift
Courage, drive, clean anger, the nerve to begin.

## Shadow
Rage. Rushing. The fight picked to avoid a feeling. Or the anger swallowed until it turns inward.

## Jungian echo
Libido as Jung first meant it: psychic energy as a whole, not only desire. The warrior, and the hero's sword that cuts the old ties. In alchemy, iron, and the fire that has to be tended at the right heat: too hot and the vessel cracks.

## Myths
- durga-buffalo: When no god could defeat the buffalo demon, their gathered fire became Durga, who rode out on a lion (Indian, Hindu)
- tyr-hand: Tyr puts his hand in the wolf Fenrir's mouth as a pledge so the gods can bind him, and loses it (Norse)
- cu-chulainn-vats: Cú Chulainn comes home from battle so hot with rage that he's plunged into three vats of cold water, and the first one bursts (Irish)
- ogun-road: Ogun clears the first road through the forest with his iron blade so the other gods can reach the earth (Yoruba)

## Images
A forge at night. A red kite over a field. An axe in a chopping block. A struck match. A spear planted in the ground. Nettles. A horse pawing at a gate. Iron filings on paper.

## Readings

### mountain-hand · synchronicity
Splitting rounds is mostly in the reading, not the swing. You look for the check in the end grain, set the maul there, and let the weight do it. Swing angry and you bury the head in the round and stand there yanking at it. Mars is the swing. He wants to move, and he's right; wood doesn't split itself. But he works best when he's looked first. Where's the check in the grain of what you're facing? Set the maul there. Then write about what it took to swing.

### howler · trickster
Mars the red-faced, Mars the elbows-out, Mars honking in traffic, Mars who stubbed his toe and cursed the whole furniture industry! Mars who ran up the hill for no reason, Mars who finally said no out loud, Mars who started the thing everyone said was too late to start! Holy the anger that told the truth. Holy the anger that was only tired. Get it on the page, the petty and the righteous, and then circle the one that's actually about love.

### fairy-tale-reader · imaginal
In the tales, the third son is always told to sit by the fire while his brothers ride out. He's slow, he's a fool, he gets the worst horse. Then he picks up the rusty sword nobody wanted, and it fits his hand. Anger works like that. We keep it in the ashes until it's needed, and then it's either rusted through or ready. Which is yours? Where have you been sitting by the fire? And what did you dream?

### nun-at-the-sea · synchronicity
Anger has a lot of energy in it, and the energy isn't the problem. The trouble starts with the story we pour it into, the one that says I'm right and they must pay. Try this: next time the heat comes up, drop the story and feel the heat. Where is it? The chest, the hands, the jaw? It's sharp and alive, and it passes if you let it be only heat. Mars is that fire before it has a target. Write about the last time you felt it, and what you did with it.

### pond-walker · imaginal
A wren was scolding a cat this morning, a bird smaller than my thumb shouting at something a hundred times its size, and the cat, astonishingly, left. I've thought about it all day. Mars isn't only the soldier. He's the wren too, the small fierce thing that won't stand for it. Imagine that wren on your shoulder the next time you need to say no. It's a lovely thought. And then the question: what have you let sit on your doorstep that you should have shouted at?

### tavern-mystic · trickster
Mars stomped into the tavern spoiling for a fight, and nobody would give him one, so he arm-wrestled the bar, and the bar won. We laughed until he laughed too. Beloved, your fire is precious, but it has been wasting itself on parking tickets and slow queues and the neighbour's hedge. Bring it here and spend it on something worth the heat. Write down the one thing that deserves your whole fierce heart, and cross off three that don't.

## Figure prompts

### synchronicity
- Where is your energy going lately, and is it where you'd send it?
- What made you angry recently, and what did the anger know that you didn't?
- What are you ready to start that you've been circling?

### imaginal
- A rider in red waits at your gate with a spare horse. Where does he want to take you?
- The Smith is working iron at the forge in you. What is he making, and how hot is the fire?
- Your anger takes a shape and sits across from you. Describe it. What does it want to protect?

### trickster
- The dice came up Mars. Write a battle cry for something utterly ordinary you have to do.
- Pick a small fight on paper with an idea you've outgrown. Win it.
- Describe your desire as a weather front moving in.

## Wild cards
- What would you do today if you weren't afraid of anyone's reaction?
- Name one thing you want, bluntly, in five words or fewer.
- Where are you swallowing a "no"?
- List what you'd set on fire if nothing could burn but the idea of it.
- Which of your fears is really a desire wearing armour?
- Describe the last time you moved your body until you couldn't think.
