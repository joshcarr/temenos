# Uranus: the Lightning-Bringer, the Unbinder

## Core
Awakening, freedom, the sudden. The break in the pattern.

## Gift
Originality, insight, liberation, the lightning-flash of seeing.

## Shadow
Restlessness. Rebellion for its own sake. The cold detachment that calls itself freedom. Breaking what didn't need breaking.

## Jungian echo
Tarnas's Prometheus the Awakener, who steals fire and pays for it. The trickster in its liberating mood. The intuition that cracks a stuck situation open. Jung's *enantiodromia*: when an attitude grows too one-sided, the psyche swings suddenly into its opposite.

## Myths
- prometheus-fire: Prometheus carries fire down from the gods in a fennel stalk and gives it to people, and is chained to a rock for it (Greek)
- psyche-lamp: Psyche lifts a lamp to see the husband she's forbidden to look at, and a drop of hot oil wakes him (Roman)
- monkey-king: The Monkey King eats the peaches of immortality, rubs his name out of the ledger of death and turns heaven upside down (Chinese)
- emperors-clothes: A child in the crowd says out loud that the emperor is wearing nothing at all (Danish, Hans Christian Andersen)

## Images
A lightning strike over the sea. A key snapping in a lock. A kite with a cut string. A spark from a doorknob. A new window in an old wall. A comet. A fennel stalk carrying fire. A birdcage with its door open.

## Readings

### engraver · imaginal
The Lightning-Bringer does not knock. He comes through the wall where the wall was thinnest, and the house of habit stands open to the weather. Prometheus carried fire down the mountain in the hollow of a fennel stalk, and they chained him to a rock for it, and he would do it again. What is kept is not always what is alive. Somewhere in you a gate has been rusted shut for years. Put your hand on the latch. Write what is on the other side.

### howler · synchronicity
Uranus the zigzag, Uranus the bolt out of a clear blue Tuesday, the phone call, the idea in the shower, the quitting, the new haircut, the move across the country at forty-five! Holy the break in the pattern, holy the one who says it out loud at dinner, holy the child pointing at the naked emperor! Something in you is tired of the rerun. Write down every time lately you surprised yourself. Then write the one you're scared to.

### mythographer · trickster
The Monkey King ate the peaches of immortality, drank the emperor's wine, rubbed his own name out of the ledger of death, and turned the palace of heaven upside down for fun. It took the Buddha himself to stop him, and even then the monkey was sure he'd reached the edge of the world when he was only standing in the Buddha's palm. The awakener in us is brilliant and a little ridiculous. Where are you rebelling for real, and where are you just knocking over the peaches?

## Figure prompts

### synchronicity
- What surprised you about yourself recently?
- Which pattern in your life is asking to be broken, and which is actually holding you up?
- Where do you feel most free, and when were you last there?

### imaginal
- Someone has left a window open in a room you keep locked. Who opened it, and what's blowing in?
- The Unbinder hands you a pair of shears. What's the one thread you'd cut?
- A stranger in you has just arrived and doesn't know your rules. What do they notice first?

### trickster
- The dice came up Uranus. Write your plans for tomorrow backwards.
- Break one rule of grammar on purpose for a whole paragraph.
- Invent a holiday that celebrates something you're not supposed to be proud of.

## Wild cards
- What would you change tomorrow if nobody you know could see?
- Write one line of a love letter to something you're not supposed to love. Don't sign it.
- What do you know that you haven't admitted yet?
- Which of your opinions would your fifteen-year-old self find boring?
- Describe the last time your life changed in an afternoon.
- If you disappeared for a year and came back different, what would be different?
