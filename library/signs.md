# The signs

A short image for each sign, used to flavour the sky line under the figure's name and, later, the readings. Element gives the form in the Plate image; modality is kept for M3.

## Aries
element: fire
modality: cardinal
rulers: mars
image: the ram at the gate, head down, first through

## Taurus
element: earth
modality: fixed
rulers: venus
image: the bull in the spring meadow, slow and certain

## Gemini
element: air
modality: mutable
rulers: mercury
image: the twins who finish each other's sentences

## Cancer
element: water
modality: cardinal
rulers: moon
image: the crab carrying its house to the tide line

## Leo
element: fire
modality: fixed
rulers: sun
image: the lion asleep in the heat of the afternoon

## Virgo
element: earth
modality: mutable
rulers: mercury
image: the gleaner walking the harvested field

## Libra
element: air
modality: cardinal
rulers: venus
image: the scales held level in a steady hand

## Scorpio
element: water
modality: fixed
rulers: mars, pluto
image: the scorpion under the stone, and the eagle above it

## Sagittarius
element: fire
modality: mutable
rulers: jupiter
image: the archer whose arrow is still in the air

## Capricorn
element: earth
modality: cardinal
rulers: saturn
image: the sea-goat climbing out of the water toward the peak

## Aquarius
element: air
modality: fixed
rulers: saturn, uranus
image: the water-bearer pouring for everyone

## Pisces
element: water
modality: mutable
rulers: jupiter, neptune
image: two fish tied by one cord, swimming opposite ways
