# Dusk anchors

Your seven original evening prompts, re-cut through the figures. The dusk rule holds for every line: ask for the image, the figure, the feeling or the pattern, never the chronology. Nothing here should be answerable with a list of what happened.

The same prompts serve Yesterday's Dusk. There, the app shifts "today" to "yesterday", "tomorrow" to "today", and uses a past-day threshold such as "What looks different about yesterday now that you've slept on it?"

## wins
original: Three wins
kin: sun, mars

### saturn
- Three wins, and which inner figure earned each one.
- [imaginal] Name one thing that held today because it was built right earlier. Draw it as a small stone structure.

### moon
- Three wins nobody saw, one of them a feeling you let yourself have.
- [synchronicity] Which win today felt good in the body? Where exactly?

### mercury
- Three wins, each told as a single line of dialogue.
- [trickster] Award today three medals in three unlikely categories.

## learned
original: One thing I learned
kin: mercury, jupiter

### saturn
- What did today teach you the slow way?
- [imaginal] The Old King marks today's lesson in his ledger. What's the one line he writes?

### moon
- What did a feeling teach you today that your mind hadn't worked out?
- [synchronicity] What did today show you about what you need?

### mercury
- What did today teach you that you didn't ask to learn?
- [trickster] Write today's lesson as a proverb from a country that doesn't exist.

## delight
original: Favourite thing of my day
kin: venus

### saturn
- Where did delight get past your guard today?
- [imaginal] What small, well-made thing gave you pleasure today? Draw it the way a craftsman would.

### moon
- Where did delight find you today, and did you let it in?
- What taste, texture or sound from today would you keep in a jar?

### mercury
- What made you laugh today, even a little? Draw the shape of the joke.
- [trickster] Your favourite moment is applying to be kept as a memory. Write its cover letter in three lines.

## story
original: Short story of a moment today
kin: mercury, moon

### saturn
- Tell one moment from today as a fable about a builder, in three sentences starting "Once...". What did the builder refuse to rush?
- [imaginal] Carve one moment from today onto a stone that will outlast you. Ten words.

### moon
- Tell one moment as a fable in three sentences, starting "Once...". Who was the helper?
- [imaginal] Tell a moment from today as a bedtime story for the child in you. Make sure it ends safely.

### mercury
- Tell one moment from today from the point of view of an object in the room.
- [trickster] Tell a moment from today as a myth in which you're the minor god of something ridiculous.

## better-tomorrow
original: One thing I could have done to make today better, and how can I apply it tomorrow?
kin: saturn, chiron

### saturn
- Where did you miss the mark, and what would a kind elder say about it?
- [imaginal] The Stonemason looks at a crooked stone from today. Does he pull it out, or build around it?

### moon
- Where did you run low today, and what would have refilled you sooner?
- [synchronicity] What feeling did you override today? What was it trying to protect?

### mercury
- Which sentence from today would you change in a second draft? Write the new one.
- [trickster] Write a short apology from your busy mind to your body. Let the body reply.

## resisting
original: Am I resisting something? What?
kin: saturn, pluto

### saturn
- Is something being resisted? Draw it as a creature. What does it guard?
- [imaginal] Where's the wall in you right now? Walk along it. Is it keeping something out, or in?

### moon
- What feeling have you kept at arm's length? Let it sit at the end of the bed and describe it.
- [synchronicity] Where did your body brace today? What was it bracing against?

### mercury
- What are you talking around? Write the sentence you keep not saying.
- [trickster] Your resistance has hired a lawyer. Let the lawyer make the case in four lines.

## leverage
original: What could I do tomorrow that is high leverage (high upside, low downside)?
kin: jupiter

### saturn
- What one stone, set tomorrow, would make the next month easier to build?
- [imaginal] The Old King lends you one hour of his patience for tomorrow. Where do you spend it?

### moon
- What small act of care tomorrow would ripple furthest, toward someone else or toward you?
- [synchronicity] How do you want tomorrow to feel, and what one thing would start that feeling early?

### mercury
- Which single message, sent tomorrow, would open the most doors?
- What small act tomorrow would the Tavern Mystic buy a round for?
