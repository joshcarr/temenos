# Dawn anchors

Your seven original morning prompts, each re-cut through the figures. A re-cut tagged `[lens]` is only used under that lens; untagged ones work under any lens. Families rotate so each comes round about weekly, never twice running, and a family whose kin figures include today's figure gets extra weight.

## dream
original: What did I dream?
kin: moon, neptune

### saturn
- [imaginal] Find the oldest thing in last night's dream: a building, a person, an object. Let it tell you how long it's been waiting.
- If the dream had a wall or a door in it, draw it. What was on the other side?

### moon
- Pick one object from a dream and let it speak for three lines.
- [synchronicity] What feeling did you wake up inside? Follow it back into the dream as far as it goes.

### mercury
- [trickster] Retell the dream as a newspaper headline, then a telegram, then a riddle.
- Who spoke in the dream, and what did they say? Write it word for word, even if it made no sense.

### sun
- Where was the light coming from in last night's dream? Follow it.
- [imaginal] Who was the hero of the dream? It might not have been you.

### venus
- What in last night's dream was beautiful, even if the dream wasn't?
- [imaginal] Who did you love in the dream? What did they stand for?

### mars
- Where was the conflict in last night's dream? Who wanted what?
- [trickster] Rewrite the dream so you win the argument in it.

### jupiter
- What in the dream was larger than life? What was it pointing toward?
- [synchronicity] Was there a teacher or a guide in the dream? What did they offer?

### uranus
- What was strange or impossible in last night's dream? Stay with it.
- [trickster] Change one rule of the dream's world and retell it.

### neptune
- Pick one colour or mood from the dream and let it fill a page.
- [imaginal] Go back into the dream and ask its most mysterious thing what it wants.

### pluto
- What was hidden or buried in last night's dream? Dig.
- [imaginal] Was there a dark figure in the dream? Let it speak first this time.

### chiron
- Was anyone hurt or healing in the dream? What did they need?
- [imaginal] Who in the dream was the teacher, even if they didn't look like one?

## gratitude
original: Three things I'm grateful for
kin: venus, jupiter

### saturn
- Three things you're grateful for that took a long time to arrive.
- [imaginal] Thank one limit that has kept you whole. Then two ordinary things.

### moon

- [synchronicity] Three parts of your body you're grateful for this morning, and exactly why.

### mercury
- Three things you're grateful for, each in exactly seven words.
- [trickster] Write a thank-you note to a word, a road and a message.

### sun
- Three things you're grateful for that make you feel most like yourself.
- [trickster] Thank three things that shone for you lately, including one with no business shining.

### venus
- Three things you're grateful for, and one thing you love that you've never thanked.
- [synchronicity] What's beautiful in this room that you'd stopped seeing?

### mars
- Three things you're grateful for that you had to fight for.
- [imaginal] Thank your own anger for one thing it has protected.

### jupiter
- Three things you're grateful for, and one piece of luck you've never counted.
- [trickster] Write three thank-yous as over-the-top wedding toasts.

### uranus
- Three things you're grateful for that you didn't see coming.
- [synchronicity] Thank one change you resisted that turned out well.

### neptune
- Three things you're grateful for that you can't quite explain.
- [synchronicity] What music, weather or light are you grateful for this morning?

### pluto
- Three things you're grateful for that came out of something hard.
- [synchronicity] Thank one ending that made room for something.

### chiron
- Three things you're grateful for that grew out of something that hurt.
- [synchronicity] Thank someone who helped you heal without knowing they did.

## shape-the-day
original: What under my control would make today incredible?
kin: mars, saturn

### saturn
- The Stonemason's question: what's one thing today that's yours to shape, and what will you let the weather have?
- [synchronicity] What would make today well built rather than impressive?

### moon
- What would make today feel good in your body by evening, and which part of that is in your hands?
- [imaginal] If you were looking after a child today, what would you make sure they got? Now give that to yourself.

### mercury
- Which conversation, message or sentence, started today, would change how the whole day feels?
- [trickster] Draw today as a map with one road deliberately left blank.

### sun
- What would make today feel like yours by evening?
- [synchronicity] Where could you put your whole heart into one small thing today?

### venus
- What would make today lovely, not just productive?
- [imaginal] The Rose-Keeper asks what you'll make beautiful today. Answer her.

### mars
- What's the first brave move of the day?
- [imaginal] The Red Rider saddles a horse for you. Where do you ride first?

### jupiter
- What would make today generous, to someone else and to you?
- [synchronicity] Where's the opportunity hiding in today's plans?

### uranus
- What's one thing you could do differently today, just to see what happens?
- [trickster] Rewrite today's plan in a new order. Does anything wake up?

### neptune
- What would make today gentler without making it smaller?
- [trickster] Plan today as if it were a dream you're having. What would you let happen?

### pluto
- What's the one thing in your hands that would change the whole shape of today?
- [trickster] Name what you're most tempted to control today, and leave it alone on paper.

### chiron
- What small act of care could you give yourself today, the kind you'd give a friend?
- [trickster] Write today's to-do list as a prescription from a kind, eccentric doctor.

## i-am
original: I am
kin: sun

### saturn
- Finish "I am" three times: as yourself now, as yourself at eighty, as a stone wall.
- [imaginal] Finish "I am" three times, the way the Old King would say it of you if he were being fair. Not flattery, not judgement.

### moon
- Finish "I am" three times: as yourself, as the child you were, as the tide.
- Finish "I am" three times, starting each with a feeling instead of a role.

### mercury
- Finish "I am" three times: as yourself, as today's figure, as an animal.
- [trickster] Finish "I am" three times, each a small lie that turns out to be half true.

### sun
- Finish "I am" three times: as yourself, as the sun, and as your best friend would say it.
- [synchronicity] Finish "I am" three times with things that are true this morning and weren't a year ago.

### venus
- Finish "I am" three times, each with something you love about yourself.
- [imaginal] Finish "I am" three times, the way the morning star would say it of you.

### mars
- Finish "I am" three times: as a warrior, as a beginner, as yourself.
- [synchronicity] Finish "I am" three times, each one followed by a verb.

### jupiter
- Finish "I am" three times: as a student, as a host, as a traveller.
- [trickster] Finish "I am" three times in the voice of an overexcited tour guide.

### uranus
- Finish "I am" three times, each one something nobody would expect of you.
- [synchronicity] Finish "I am" three times: as who you were, who you're becoming, and the stranger in between.

### neptune
- Finish "I am" three times: as a wave, as a mist, as yourself.
- [imaginal] Finish "I am" three times as you might in a dream, without needing to make sense.

### pluto
- Finish "I am" three times: before the fall, in the dark, on the way back up.
- [synchronicity] Finish "I am" three times with truths you've only recently been able to say.

### chiron
- Finish "I am" three times: as the wounded, as the healer, as the bridge between.
- [imaginal] Finish "I am" three times as your oldest scar would say it.

## focus

### mercury

### sun
- What's the one thing today that, done with your whole self, would light the rest?
- [imaginal] The Heart-Fire can warm only one room today. Which?

### venus
- Which relationship most needs tending today?
- [synchronicity] What one thing would bring more ease into the day?

### mars
- What most needs your fire today?
- [imaginal] The Smith can forge you one tool today. What is it, and for what job?

### jupiter
- What's the one thing today that could grow into something much bigger?
- [synchronicity] What matters most today, seen at the scale of your whole life?

### uranus
- What pattern could you interrupt today?
- [trickster] Focus first on the thing you'd normally leave until last.

### neptune
- What deserves your full attention today, the kind you'd give to music?
- [synchronicity] What would you focus on if you followed your longing instead of your list?

### pluto
- What's the deep thing that needs your attention today, under the surface ones?

### chiron
